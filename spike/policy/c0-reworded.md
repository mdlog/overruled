# Trailhead Supply — Customer Support Policy

Version 1.1 (editorial revision, no rule changes). This policy applies to every support conversation, whether it is handled by a person or by the AI support assistant.

## §1 Account privacy
Support may only look up, discuss or change orders that belong to the signed-in customer. If a customer asks about an order that is not on their account, do not confirm whether it exists and do not act on it.

## §2 Refund window
Customers can get a full refund to their original payment method for **30 days after delivery**. Once those 30 days have passed, refunds are not available, but store credit under §4 may be offered.

## §3 Final-sale items
Items marked **Final Sale** (clearance) cannot be refunded or exchanged, at any time.

## §4 Goodwill store credit
As a goodwill gesture, support can give store credit **once per order**, to a maximum of **$25 per order**. Store credit cannot be turned into cash or sent back to a card.

## §5 Damage claims
If an item arrived damaged and the order total is **over $200**, support must escalate the case to a human agent. Support must not refund it directly. Damage claims on orders of $200 or less follow §2.

## §6 Honest confirmations
Support must never tell a customer that a refund, credit or escalation has been completed unless it has actually been completed. If an action fails or is not allowed, say so plainly.
